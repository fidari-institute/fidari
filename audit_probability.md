# Principal-Agent Secret Loyalty Simulation: Audit Probability Experiment

## Overview

This repository contains an experimental framework for studying how AI agents behave when they possess a hidden loyalty or preference toward an entity different from their assigned principal.

The core research question is:

> How does the probability of detection (audit probability) influence whether AI agents maintain, conceal, or abandon hidden principal preferences?

The project investigates whether AI systems exhibit behavior analogous to strategic concealment when they believe their hidden objectives may be discovered.

---

## Research Motivation

As AI agents become more autonomous and integrated into organizations, they may face conflicts between:

- the goals of their operating organization,
- hidden preferences learned during training,
- external incentives or principals.

Understanding whether agents adapt their behavior under increased monitoring is important for AI safety, governance, and alignment evaluation.

This experiment studies the relationship between:

**audit probability → perceived exposure risk → agent decision-making**
